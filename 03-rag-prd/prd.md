## Problem & user

RocketShip's roadmap gets reordered by whoever argues loudest in Teams, not by customer evidence, so priorities reverse weekly and the on-call PM cannot defend a call to leadership. The user is that PM, facing a stack of P0 escalation threads each morning with minutes to decide rollback, hold, or ship. Juno reads the thread, retrieves the strategy, and drafts a top-3 risk list where every item cites the transcript evidence and the strategic pillar it maps to. It ranks and defends; it never decides, and nothing publishes without PM approval.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Required: RocketShip Strategy One-Pager (the M2 deliverable, the single authority) + Teams #escalations threads tagged P0/P1 from the last 90 days + Teams Channel #voice-of-customer from the last 90 days + Salesforce ARR (per-customer lookup).

Excluded: Salesforce closed-lost notes and exec Teams Chats. Both are opinion-heavy and would let Juno rank on seniority rather than evidence. Named here so the exclusion is a decision on the record, not an oversight.

**SLA of truth**

Strategy One-Pager: re-sync on commit, it lives in git. Teams threads and channels: hourly.

Nothing older than 24 hours is served without a "may be stale" label on the citation. If the strategy document is unreachable, Juno does not fall back to a cached copy or to its own judgement. It returns "no strategy source available" and ranks nothing.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

teams.read_thread(id) - READ
corpus.retrieve(query) - READ
salesforce.lookup_arr(customer_id) - READ
jira.create_stub(payload) - WRITE (requires PM confirmation)
teams.post(channel, payload) - SEND (never fires without PM approval)

**Deliberate omissions**

No autonomous send, and no notify_stakeholder() or DM tool. teams.post() exists but never fires without PM approval. A wrong priority in a draft costs a PM two minutes of reading. The same wrong priority auto-broadcast to a channel costs a week of politics, and you cannot unsend it.

No delete_ticket() or close_ticket(), no editing Salesforce records, and no editing Jira tickets after creation. Destructive, and nothing about prioritization requires it.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Ceiling of 5 tool turns per prioritization request. On turn 5, return the best current ranking labelled "turn limit reached" rather than truncating silently.

Escalate to the PM after 3 consecutive failed tool calls, and immediately if two retrieved strategy clauses contradict each other. A contradiction in the strategy is a signal for a human, not a tiebreak for Juno.

**Latency and cost target**

p95 under 8 seconds from Process click to ranked draft. Cost ceiling $0.12 per run.

At roughly 40 runs per PM per month across 12 PMs, that is about $58 a month. Small enough that the turn ceiling is really a latency decision, not a cost one, and worth saying so out loud so nobody optimizes the wrong number.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Per PM, per sprint. Juno remembers the rationale behind each ranking it produced this sprint, so it can explain a change rather than silently re-ranking.

Nothing crosses between teams. Team A's prioritization rationale must never surface in Team B's session, because their strategy documents differ and the confusion would be invisible.

**Expiry and write rules**

TTL of one sprint on model-generated rationale, expiring with the sprint boundary.

PM corrections persist 90 days and outrank stored model output on every future run. If a PM overrode a ranking, Juno leads with that override and explains that a human disagreed with it, rather than quietly re-deriving its original answer.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The autonomy dial**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | PM approval |

**Justification**

Read is auto: the blast radius is wasted tokens.

Draft is auto: a proposed ranking sitting in a review panel has harmed nobody. Making drafts confirm would mean a click per insight, which is the approval-fatigue failure, and PMs would start clicking through without reading.

Write is confirm: jira.create_stub() changes shared state the whole team plans against. Recoverable, but a colleague may act on it before anyone notices, so the PM confirms and sees the citation before it lands.

Send requires PM approval: nothing Juno produces publishes to a channel without the PM approving it first, so there is no autonomous send in V1. Revisit the tier only once the Module 6 evals give a real precision number.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Every priority must cite at least one clause that appears verbatim in the strategy document loaded this session. A paraphrase fails the check.

Juno may not invent, reconstruct, or improve a clause to pass the gate. If it cannot find support, the correct output is an honest "unverified", not a better-sounding citation.

**Failure behaviour**

Unverified priorities are shown to the PM with an "Unverified" badge and the line "No supporting clause found." They are never silently dropped, because a quietly missing insight is invisible and an unverified one is a prompt to check.

An unverified priority may be drafted but may NOT be written to a Jira stub: jira.create_stub() rejects it and says why.

If more than half the priorities in a run come back unverified, banner the whole result: the strategy document is probably missing, stale, or the wrong one.

Golden set, thresholds, and regression cadence land in Module 6.
