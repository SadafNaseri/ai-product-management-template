## Trust gaps

| **The Black-box Gap** | Can the user see why the AI decided? | Noticeable but recoverable (3/5 closed) | Every ranked item ships with its reasoning visible: the transcript quote it rests on, the Source ID, and the Strategic pillar it maps to, shown as a Strategic Traceability footer on each Insight Card. ## Highest-priority fix

**The Black-box Gap** (3/5).

For a Copilot whose entire value is a ranking leadership will trust, an unexplained rank is a non-starter. If a PM cannot see why Juno ranked something, they will not defend it upward, and they will quietly route around the tool. Visibility of reasoning is therefore the gate for adoption, not a nice-to-have.

The fix: the Strategic Traceability footer on every Insight Card (evidence quote + Source ID + Strategic pillar), backed by the M3 verbatim-citation verification gate so the reason shown is a real clause from the source, not a paraphrase. Until RAG lands, the M1 prototype still surfaces the transcript quote and source with a "grounding pending" label, so the reasoning stays visible even before full citation is wired.

## Verdict

**Conditional Go.** Safe to run as a bounded pilot in Copilot mode: the PM approves every publish, and the Manual Override on each card gives a working stop. Hold on any autonomous or auto-publish behaviour until two things are true: the Black-box fix (Strategic Traceability + verbatim-citation gate) is live, and the M6 evals return a real accuracy number. Ship the assistive draft experience now; earn the autonomy later.
